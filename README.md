- 10 Challenging Campaign Operations with Advanced Boss Archetypes
- 30 Tunable Weapons (Handguns, SMGs, Rifles, Shotguns, Heavy & Energy Weapons)
- Stealth Mechanics, Dynamic Dark Illumination & Sound Spatialization
- In-Game Auto-Updater support
