# v10 Power Grid

Drop-in pack: `js/v10-power.js` (loaded after `v9-skills.js`, before `profiles.js`). No save data, no new settings.

## Circuit breakers
- 2 to 6 normal rooms per level get a wall-mounted breaker (yellow box, green LED = power on, red = off). Start, extraction and boss arena rooms never get one.
- Stand next to it and press **USE** (`E` / gamepad `X`) to cut the room's power: every lamp and trim strip in that room goes dark, the room stops counting as lit for player exposure and enemy visibility.
- Every hostile inside at the moment of the cut is **dazed for 3.5 to 5.5 s**: they stumble with a random gaze, cancel any burst / sniper charge / minigun spin / rusher lunge, drop out of combat into SEARCH and lose track of your exact position. They bark "Lights out!".
- After the daze, hostiles standing in a dark room stay half-blind: they only see you within 140 px, or 40% of their view range if you stand in a lit area, or 60% right after you fire. Their shots from the dark get 2.2x spread.
- Flipping a breaker makes a small noise (200 px). Press USE again to restore power.

## Alarm panels and runners
- 1 to 4 rooms get a red alarm panel.
- When a hostile first spots you (normal grunts, snipers and other non-heavy / non-rusher types), there is a 60% chance it sprints to the nearest panel (within 1100 px, ignoring a panel you're standing next to) instead of fighting. A red `ALARM!` tag and a dashed line show the runner when you can see it. Only one runner at a time.
- The runner needs 1.3 s at the panel. Kill it, or black out its room to daze it, and the alarm never trips. It gives up after 12 s.
- **Alarm triggered**: 35 s of siren, red rotating beacons and red emergency lamps in every room. Emergency lighting overrides every blackout (no blindness while the alarm runs). All hostiles within 1800 px get your position and engage, and 2 reinforcements (+1 per extra alarm, max 4) arrive from the spawn points nearest the panel. Reinforcements are skipped during an active boss fight.
- Hold **USE** for 1.5 s at any panel during the alarm to silence it early (+15 XP).

## Tuning
All numbers live in the `CONF` object at the top of `js/v10-power.js`, also exposed as `BO.V10.CONF` for quick live tweaking from the console.
